Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about CLENIZA sachets, refill use, daily cleaning, and business inquiries.

Are CLENIZA products safe for daily use?

Yes, our products are designed for regular household cleaning applications. Always follow the usage instructions on the pack.

Why sachet packaging?

Sachets reduce cost, improve convenience, make products more accessible, and support a refill and reuse culture.

Can I refill existing bottles?

Yes. CLENIZA sachets are designed for easy refilling into reusable bottles or containers.

Are products suitable for commercial use?

Yes, depending on the application. Offices, shops, commercial washrooms, and utility spaces can use relevant CLENIZA products.

Do you support bulk or business enquiries?

Yes. Use the contact page for product enquiries, bulk requirements, collaborations, customer support, and general business requests.
